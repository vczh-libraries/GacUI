### UIAList tool

- A new `UIAList` tool in `GacUI` repo:
  - A tree view at the left side with a button to refresh all processs, sub processes and windows, ignoring all process trees without any visible window.
  - A tree view at the right side to expand all controls with their titles.
    - Double click opens a window with a list view for all properties, and other tab pages for each actionable concept.
  - Resizable by dragging between two tree views.
