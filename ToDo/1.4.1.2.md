- **VlppOS**
  - Complete cross-platform TUI interface with typing/keyboard/mouse/wheel support, starting from:

    - `TUI::InstallListener`

  - GacUI on TUI, available on Linux and macOS.
  - GuiApplication::RefreshThemes to change theme app-wide after global theme options is changed.
