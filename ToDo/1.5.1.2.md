# 1.5.1.2

## New Feature

- **GacUI**:
  - `GuiControl` added `ContextMenu` and `DisplayContextMenuOnRightClick` properties.
  - New gesture: `GuiPointGesture_ShortPressing`, `GuiPointGesture_LongPressing`.
  - New component: `vl::presentation::compositions::MomentumScrolling`.
  - Text box controls support touch experience: left double click fallback, short press to jump, long press to select from caret to where touching.
