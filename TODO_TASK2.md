### Press offers and deferred clicks

- The actual Started fields are `generateLeftButtonClick` and `generateRightButtonClick`. Offer exactly one press kind at End, short below 500000 microseconds and long at or above 500000 microseconds.   | Enabled click flags | Short press | Long press |

  | Both | Left click | Right click |
  | Left only | Left click | Left click |
  | Right only | None | Right click |
  | Neither | None | None |
